## Purpose
Thinkspace loves dogs and is dog friendly, but dogs are only welcome under clear conditions. This page merges the member lease addendum, the FAQ, and the employee rules into one reference so you can answer "can I bring my dog?" correctly, know what it costs, and know what to do when a dog causes a problem.

## Part 1 · Members: the Dog Policy Addendum (attached to the lease)
A member who wants to bring a dog signs the Dog Policy Addendum. Each section is initialed. The addendum form collects the **dog's name, breed, age, the owner's name, signature, and date**.

### What the member agrees to
1. Pay a **one-time refundable pet fee of $300** (see the fee note below; the FAQ describes the fee differently).
2. Provide, for their tenant file: a **photograph of the dog**, **vaccine certification**, and **spay/neuter certification**.
3. Accept that Thinkspace **reserves the right to restrict the right to bring the pet at any time, at our discretion**.

### When a dog may be excluded
The dog may be excluded if it:
- causes any person allergic reactions, fear, or physical or psychological discomfort;
- causes distraction or liability to other tenants;
- fails proper grooming and hygiene;
- fails to comply with the building's dog policy.

### Dogs that may not be brought to the workplace
- **Restricted breeds or mixes:** Pitbull Terriers, Mastiffs, Rottweilers, Staffordshire Terriers, Chow Chows, Presa Canarios, Wolf-hybrids, Cane Corso. A licensed vet may be required to verify the breed.
- Dogs that are not spayed or neutered, including any dog **older than 6 months** that is not spayed or neutered.
- Unvaccinated or sick dogs, and dogs with internal or external parasites.
- Dogs that jump, chase, bite, growl, bark, or behave aggressively.
- Dogs that foul the inside or outside of the building.

### Where the dog may be and how it must be handled
- **Leashed at all times** and in continuous, full control of the owner.
- Only on the premises **in the physical presence of the owner**, and **only in the owner's office**.
- **Not permitted in common areas:** kitchen areas, meeting rooms, lobbies.
- Leashed and under control in hallways, stairways, and elevators.
- The owner cleans up completely and immediately.

### Liability and costs
- The owner covers in full any costs arising from non-compliance: building damage, cleaning fees, excessive pet hair, odor removal.
- The owner is completely and solely liable for injuries or damage caused by the animal.
- Repair, cleaning, and maintenance costs (including failure to clean up waste within property lines) are **charged in full to the next invoice, not taken from the pet deposit**.
- Thinkspace is not liable for loss of, or injury to, any animal. The owner unconditionally releases Thinkspace from liability.

## Part 2 · Member eligibility and responsibilities (from the "Rules" doc)
For a member's pet to participate, the pet must be:
- **at least 2 years old**;
- up to date on state-required vaccinations;
- free of communicable infections and parasites;
- clean and well groomed;
- house-trained;
- obedient and well-socialized, with no history of biting, barking, chasing, or aggression;
- **microchipped**;
- covered under the owner's **homeowners or renters insurance that covers dog bites**.

Pet owners are 100% responsible for the pet's behavior, well-being, and hygiene, and must:
1. Be respectful of others and pet-proof their workspace.
2. Keep the pet with them and under control at all times (or arrange for a colleague to do so).
3. Provide their own leash, crate, gate, waste bags, toys, and bowls.
4. Handle bathroom breaks, water, food, and exercise.
5. Clean up accidents immediately.
6. Maintain dog-bite insurance and remain legally and financially responsible for damage.
7. Provide annual proof of vaccination and health.
8. Keep the pet out of pet-free areas.
9. Use alternate care on days they cannot manage the pet (for example an all-day meeting) or when the pet is ill.
10. Take the pet home if it becomes a distraction, nuisance, or danger, or if asked to by their manager.

## Part 3 · FAQ: the answers you will give at the front desk

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is the in-depth legal wording? | Below the Q&A in the FAQ doc, and on each dog owner's signed contract |
| A member signed the addendum. Who gets it so the desk knows the dog is allowed? | Send it to the **front desk** |
| How do we track who pays dog rent? | It is **added to their monthly invoices** |
| Can members bring dogs to events? | **No, unless it is a service animal** |
| Can drop-in coworkers or meeting guests bring dogs? | **No, unless it is a service animal** |
| Can dogs come to events where outside people pay? | **Never, unless it is a service animal** |
| Does it cost a member anything to have a dog? | **Yes.** As a Private Office (PO) or Full-Time Coworking (FTCW) member: **$300 nonrefundable initial fee** and **$50 per month pet rent**, plus additional charges. That does **not** cover janitorial clean-up or the poop bags outside; that is **$100 per month** on top |

> **Note for the trainer:** the lease addendum calls the $300 a "one-time **refundable** pet fee", while the FAQ calls it a "$300 **nonrefundable** initial fee" and adds $50/month pet rent and a $100/month janitorial charge. Both are kept here as written. Confirm with your manager which wording is current before quoting a member.

## Part 4 · Employees who want to bring their dog
Employees sign a separate Employee Dog Policy contract (written for the office at **1700 Westlake Ave N, Suite 200, Seattle, WA 98109**). Rules:

1. A dog may be allowed if its breed, health, and behavior are acceptable and it does not affect operations or productivity. **Dogs are the only permitted pets.**
2. Required for the employee file: a **photograph**, **vaccine certification**, and **spay/neuter certification**.
3. Allowing or excluding a dog is at the full discretion of the **immediate supervisor, General Manager, and/or CEO**. A **supervisor signature** is required on the contract.
4. The same exclusion rules and restricted breeds as members apply, **plus** these additional restricted breeds for employees: **Siberian Huskies, German Shepherds, Saint Bernards**. Dogs with fleas or a communicable disease are also excluded.
5. Not permitted in common areas, **including the front desk area**.
6. The employee is liable for all injury, damage, and cleaning costs.

### Day-to-day rules for employees (from the "Rules" doc)
- **Ask your manager for permission every single time** before bringing your dog. Even when you are covering the desk in an emergency, still ask.
- Bringing a dog is a **once-in-a-blue-moon, emergency-only** thing. Most of the time the dog should **not** be at the front desk.
- **Do not feed your dog at the front desk** (the food smells).
- Dogs must be well behaved, potty trained, and quiet.
- **Dogs cannot go on sales tours.**
- **No dogs under two years of age.**

## Part 6 · When a dog causes a problem (escalation path)
1. **Talk with the pet owner** and try to resolve it mutually.
2. If that does not work, **escalate to the pet owner's supervisor**.
3. If it is still unresolved, **escalate to Human Resources**.

Remember that the owner must take the dog home if it becomes a distraction, nuisance, or danger, or if a manager asks, and that Thinkspace can restrict any dog at any time at its discretion.
