## Purpose

Consistent, warm, and clear communication is what guests remember. This SOP defines how we speak with guests in person, on the phone, and in writing.

## In person

- **The 10/5 rule:** make eye contact and smile at 10 feet; greet verbally at 5 feet.
- Use the guest's name once you know it, at least once per interaction.
- Stand to greet; never talk to a guest while seated behind a desk if you can avoid it.
- End every interaction with an offer: *"Is there anything else I can help you with?"*

## On the phone

- Answer within **three rings**.
- Standard greeting: *"Good [morning/afternoon/evening], thank you for calling [Property], this is [Name]. How may I help you?"*
- Ask permission before placing a guest on hold, and never leave them on hold more than **60 seconds** without checking back.
- Repeat back any request or reservation detail before ending the call.

## In writing (email, messaging app)

- Reply to guest messages within **15 minutes** during your shift.
- Use full sentences, no abbreviations or emoji.
- Sign with your first name and role.
- Never share another guest's information, room number, or schedule.

## Language we use / avoid

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| "Certainly, I'll take care of that." | "Yeah, sure." |
| "Let me find out for you." | "I don't know." |
| "I apologize for the inconvenience." | "That's not my department." |
| "May I have your room number?" | "What room are you in?" |

## Confidentiality

Never confirm whether someone is a guest, or give out a room number, to anyone who is not the registered guest — including people claiming to be family. Direct them to the Duty Manager.
