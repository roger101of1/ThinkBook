## Purpose

Allstate Passports is a member business whose clients come to the front desk to drop off paperwork or ask about passports. This SOP tells you exactly what to do, and what not to answer, for each type of visitor.

## Who to know

| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| **Albina** | Owner of Allstate Passports. Her phone number is on a sticker at the front desk. |

## If a client is dropping off homework or paperwork

1. Give them a **large manilla envelope**.
2. Envelopes are stored at the **front desk, top drawer, far left side** (if you are facing the elevator, where KB sits).

## If a client asks questions

1. Do **not** answer specific passport or process questions.
2. Direct them to the owner, **Albina**.
3. Albina's phone number is on a sticker at the front desk. Tell the client to reach out to her directly.

## If a walk-in client arrives

1. Give them **Albina's number** so they can reach out to her.
2. Let them know they can go to **Staples** to get a non-expedited passport application and photos.
3. If they specifically need an **expedited passport**, let them know that expedited processing is exactly what Allstate Passports specializes in.

> **Warning:** never give passport or process advice yourself. Every process question goes to Albina.
