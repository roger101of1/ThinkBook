## Purpose

Every guest request is logged, fulfilled within target, and closed with the guest. No request should ever be forgotten.

## The request loop

1. **Capture** — open a ticket on the task board with: room, guest name, request, time received, who took it.
2. **Assign** — to yourself or the right team (Housekeeping, Maintenance, Front Desk).
3. **Fulfil** — within the service-level target below.
4. **Close the loop** — confirm with the guest that it was done (in person, call, or message).
5. **Close the ticket** — with the completion time.

## Service-level targets

| Request type | Target |
|---|---|
| Extra towels, pillows, toiletries | 15 minutes |
| Room temperature / minor fix | 30 minutes |
| Wake-up call setup | Immediately |
| Restaurant / transport booking | 1 hour, or before the time needed |
| Late checkout | Answer within 10 minutes (approve up to 1:00 PM without asking) |

## What you can approve on your own

- Late checkout up to **1:00 PM**.
- Complimentary items under **$20** (drink voucher, snack, toiletry).
- Room move to the **same category** when a room is available.

Anything beyond this goes to the Duty Manager.

## Follow-up rule

If a ticket is still open at **double its target time**, personally update the guest with a revised ETA — do not wait for them to ask.
