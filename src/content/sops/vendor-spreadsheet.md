## Purpose

The Redmond Vendor List is the spreadsheet of **who we use to fix things** at the Redmond (RED) location. Use it to find the right vendor before calling anyone about a repair.

## How to use it

1. First check whether the issue is something we fix ourselves (SOP "Things We Can Fix Ourselves") or something property management must handle (SOP "Items Property Management Must Be Called to Fix").
2. If it is neither, open the Redmond Vendor List and find the vendor for that type of work.
3. Contact the vendor listed there.
