## Purpose
How to log in to the Arlo security cameras for each location.

## Accounts

| Location | Account email | Password |
|---|---|---|
| Redmond (RED) | arlo-red@thinkspace.com | **in 1Password** |
| Seattle (SEA) | arlo-sea@thinkspace.com | **in 1Password** |

## Steps

1. Go to https://www.arlo.com/en-us/login (bookmarked on the laptop).
2. Log in with the account for your location (RED or SEA) using the password from 1Password.
3. If the site will not load, remember Arlo runs on the Ziply internet connection; check the Ziply outage map.

> **Warning:** Never write the Arlo passwords in email, chat, Trello or notes. 1Password only.
