## Purpose
How your employee ID, W-9, direct deposit and payroll get set up.

## What happens

1. Your manager has **emailed Ben and Peri** to get your employee ID, W-9, auto deposit and payroll set up.
2. Watch for their reply or a request for your details (bank information for auto deposit, tax forms).
3. Reply promptly with anything they ask for. Payroll runs bi-weekly with paychecks on the 5th and 20th of the month (see the Employee Handbook), so a delay here delays your first check.
4. If you have not heard from Ben or Peri by the end of Day 1, tell your manager.

> **Note:** The separate Day 0 card "W-9, i9, and copy of ID given to Ben" covers handing Ben your W-9, I-9 and a copy of your ID. That card has no further description.
