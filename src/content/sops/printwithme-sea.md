## Purpose
Where the Seattle PrintWithMe login lives.

## SOP
1. The **Seattle PrintWithMe login lives in 1Password**; the card links to a 1Password share (below). Never copy the login or the staff promo code into email, chat, or this page.
2. If the share link has expired or you cannot see the item, ask your manager to re-share it to your 1Password account.
3. For how to actually print with PrintWithMe (selecting a printer, entering "Thinkspace" as the location, using the staff promo code), see the PrintWithMe SOP in the Technical Skills module.
