## Purpose

How to create, price, send for signature, and activate a Virtual Office or Coworking contract in the Kube portal, from finding the member to the charges generated on activation.

## Step 1 · Locate the company or user

1. Click the **magnifying glass** in the upper-right corner of the Kube portal.
2. Type the name of the person or company.
3. Click the magnifying glass to the right of the search bar, or press **Enter/Return**.
4. Look for them under the **Company**, **Member**, or **Lead** tab.

**If you can't find them:** tick the **Include Archived** checkbox and search again.

**If you still can't find them:** click **Add User** to add the company and its primary contact as a member or lead.

**Once you've found them:** click **Create Contract**.

## Step 2 · Terms & Company page

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| Property | Update if needed |
| Contract Name | Update as needed |
| Move-in Date | Select it. **The Move-in Date controls the proration of the first month's rent and when any booking credits activate.** |
| Contract Term | Enter **6** or **12** based on the agreement |
| Term Type | **Fixed Term** |
| Auto Renew | **On** |
| Renewal Price | Should default to **Custom Pricing** with a **4.00% increase** |
| Bill Full Contract Cost at Start of Term | Tick only if the member must be invoiced for the entire term up front (see warning) |

> **Warning — one-way option.** Once *Bill Full Contract Cost at Start of Term* is enabled on an active contract, it **cannot be disabled through an amendment**. You would have to terminate the contract and create a new one without the option.

Click **Save & Continue**.

> After Save & Continue the contract **auto-saves** as you move through the following pages — but **if you change anything on the Terms & Company page again, you must click Save & Continue** to save and apply those changes.

## Step 3 · Add the space(s)

You should be redirected to the **Spaces** tab in the **Products & Services** section.

1. Click **Add to Contract** next to the applicable package(s).
2. Click the **pencil icon** to edit the space price, setup fee and/or security deposit.
3. Choose a pricing mode:

**Simple pricing** — one price for the whole term. Fields: Price, % Discount, % Increase, $ Discount, No Discount, plus Security Deposit and Setup.
*Manual example: Office 126 · Retail $875.00 · Contract $875.00 · Security Deposit $875.00 · Setup $0.00 · Monthly.*

**Custom pricing** — price steps by date range. Click **Add Step** for each period; each step has From / To dates, Retail Price, Contract Price and an Adjustment (e.g. *Free*, *Contract Price*).
*Manual example: 05/2025 → 05/2025 = Free ($0.00); 06/2025 → 05/2026 = Contract Price ($875.00).*

4. Click **Save**.
5. Once all spaces are added, click **Amenities** in the menu on the left.

## Step 4 · Add booking credits and monthly amenities

1. Click **Add to Contract** next to the **"4 Conference Room Hours/Month"** amenity.
   > **Seattle:** skip this step — there are no booking credits at the Seattle property.
2. Add and update any other monthly amenities/charges based on the agreement (e.g. parking).
   - Use the **QTY** adjustment (– / +) to set the quantity.
   - Use **Simple** or **Custom** pricing to set the monthly or one-time price. Occurrence can be *One-Time* or *Monthly*; tick *Free Amenity* if it's included.
   *Manual example: Parking (Paid) · Qty 1 · $85.00 · Monthly.*
3. Click **Save**.
4. Once everything is added, click **Communicate** in the menu on the left.

## Step 5 · Have all parties sign the agreement

Choose a **Method of Communication**:

### ySign (e-signature)

1. Enter notes in the **Notes** box — these notes appear on the agreement.
2. Use the **Default Template** *(in progress)*.
3. Use **Append Document Upload** to add additional PDF documents to the end of the Agreement PDF.
4. Click **Send**.
5. Click **Download** at any time to get the most recent version (signatures included if done). The **ySign Timeline** shows: Contract Created → Contract Sent to Company → Company Signature → Online Countersignature → ySign Complete.

> **Note from the manual:** the Countersign and Activate steps for ySign will be sent after the ySign document template is ready and training is complete.

### Manual (no signature needed, or signed by hand)

1. Enter notes in the **Notes** box.
2. Use the **Default Template** *(in progress)*.
3. Use **Signed Document Upload** to upload a copy of the hand-signed agreement. Once uploaded it can be downloaded or removed.
4. Click **Email** to send the agreement to the member/prospect.
5. Click **Download** at any time for the most recent version.
6. Click **Activate** in the upper-right corner.

## Step 6 · Activate the contract

> **Manual Method of Communication ONLY for now.**

1. Check the checkbox to the left of the charge(s).
   > If **no charges are listed**, the Move-in Date is after the next Monthly Posting date — Kube will add that month's contract charges then.
2. Update the **Creation Date** and **Due Date** if needed.
   > **The Due Date is when the first automatic payment attempt is made** if the company has a payment method on file and auto-pay is enabled.
3. Click **Activate Contract**, then **OK** in the pop-up to confirm.

### What you'll see after activation

*Charges Generated on Activation* lists each item with its charge code and posting month, e.g. Office 126 → Rent → $875.00 → May 2025; Parking (Paid) → Parking → pro-rated $32.90 → May 2025. *Charges Due on Activation* shows the pro-rated first month plus security deposit (manual example: $907.90 total), and *Monthly Charges* shows the recurring subtotal ($85.00 in the example).

## Quick checks

- Right tab when searching? Include Archived before Add User.
- Move-in Date correct? It drives proration and booking credits.
- 6 or 12 · Fixed Term · Auto Renew On · Custom Pricing +4.00%.
- Full-term billing really intended? It can't be undone by amendment.
- Changed Terms & Company after the first save? Click Save & Continue again.
- Seattle: no booking credits.
- Activation: Manual method only; check the charges; confirm the Due Date.
