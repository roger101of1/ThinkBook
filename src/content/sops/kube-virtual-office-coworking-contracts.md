## Purpose

How to create a Virtual Office or Coworking contract in the Kube portal, from finding the member to saving the Terms & Company page.

## Step 1 · Locate the company or user

1. Click the **magnifying glass** in the upper-right corner of the Kube portal.
2. Type the name of the person or company.
3. Click the magnifying glass to the right of the search bar, or press **Enter/Return**.
4. Look for them under the **Company**, **Member**, or **Lead** tab.

**If you can't find them:** tick the **Include Archived** checkbox and search again.

**If you still can't find them:** click **Add User** to add the company and its primary contact as a member or lead.

**Once you've found them:** click **Create Contract**.

## Step 2 · Fill out the Terms & Company page

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| Property | Update if needed |
| Contract Name | Update as needed |
| Move-in Date | Select it. **The Move-in Date controls the proration of the first month's rent and when any booking credits activate.** |
| Contract Term | Enter **6** or **12** based on the agreement |
| Term Type | **Fixed Term** |
| Auto Renew | **On** |
| Renewal Price | Should default to **Custom Pricing** with a **4.00% increase** |
| Bill Full Contract Cost at Start of Term | Tick only if the member must be invoiced for the entire term up front (see warning below) |

> **Warning — one-way option.** Once *Bill Full Contract Cost at Start of Term* is enabled on an active contract, it **cannot be disabled through an amendment**. You would have to terminate the contract and create a new one without the option.

Then click **Save & Continue**.

> **Note:** after Save & Continue, the contract **auto-saves** as you move through the following pages.

## Quick checks before you continue

- Right tab? (Company / Member / Lead — and Include Archived if they're missing)
- Move-in date correct? It drives proration and booking credits.
- Term 6 or 12, Fixed Term, Auto Renew On, Custom Pricing +4.00%.
- Did you really mean to bill the full term up front? It can't be undone by amendment.
