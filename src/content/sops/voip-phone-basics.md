## Purpose
Who to call when a VOIP phone has a problem, and what to do when a member asks for a phone in their office.

## Phone problems

| Situation | Who helps | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Any phone issue (VOIP) | **Skynet** | **360-802-6657** |

Skynet is the vendor who supports our phones. Call them for any phone issue rather than trying to fix the hardware yourself.

## A member wants a phone in their office

1. Do not promise a phone or a price on the spot.
2. Let **KB, Hannah or Abby** know about the request. They will help the member get a phone set up.
