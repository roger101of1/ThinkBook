## Purpose
What equipment is in the conference rooms and where the spare cables live, so you can help members get set up.

## The amenities

| Amenity | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Meeting Owl** | The 360-degree camera/speaker/mic device for video calls. Training on it is on the "Items to be trained on" list. |
| **TVs with HDMI** | Each conference room TV connects over HDMI. |
| **Cables** | Spare cables are kept in **storage in the IT closet**. |

## Steps when a member needs help

1. For video calls, set up the Meeting Owl and connect it to their laptop.
2. For screen sharing, connect their laptop to the TV with an HDMI cable.
3. If a cable is missing or broken, fetch a replacement from the IT closet storage.
4. Reset the room afterwards (cables tidy, Owl back in place) as part of the daily meeting room reset.
