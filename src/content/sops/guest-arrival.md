## Purpose

Deliver a smooth, personal arrival for every guest, and make sure every check-in is complete and compliant.

## Before the guest arrives (pre-arrival, day of)

1. Pull the **arrivals list** at the start of shift.
2. For each arrival, check: reservation notes, special requests, VIP flag, estimated arrival time.
3. Confirm room status with Housekeeping on the task board — target is **"Inspected"** by 2:00 PM for all arrivals.
4. Prepare welcome amenities for VIP and returning guests.

## When the guest arrives

1. Greet (see *Guest Communication Standards*) and welcome them by name if you can identify them.
2. Ask for the name on the reservation and a **government-issued photo ID**.
3. Verify: dates, room type, rate, and number of guests. Read them back.
4. Take a payment method on file for incidentals. Explain the hold amount.
5. Explain: Wi-Fi, breakfast hours, checkout time (**11:00 AM**), and how to reach you.
6. Hand over keys in a key packet with the room number **written, not spoken aloud**.
7. Offer help with luggage and directions to the room.

## If the room is not ready

- Apologize once, sincerely. Offer to store luggage and give an honest ETA.
- Offer a complimentary drink voucher (no approval needed).
- If the wait will exceed **45 minutes**, notify the Duty Manager to discuss an upgrade or compensation.
- Text/call the guest the moment the room is ready.

## After check-in

- Update the reservation status to **Checked-in** immediately.
- Log any special requests as tickets.
- Note anything the next shift should know in the handover notes.

## Common mistakes

- Saying the room number out loud in the lobby.
- Skipping the ID check for "regulars" — it is required every time.
- Forgetting to confirm the number of guests (affects fire safety counts).
