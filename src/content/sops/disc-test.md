## Purpose
Take the DISC assessment as part of your personal development during onboarding.

## What it is

DISC is a behavioral-style assessment that describes how you tend to work and communicate across four traits: **D**ominance, **I**nfluence, **S**teadiness and **C**onscientiousness. The card links to the free Tony Robbins DISC assessment. Knowing your style, and recognizing others', helps you adapt how you talk with members, vendors and teammates.

## Steps

1. Open the Tony Robbins DISC link below and complete the free assessment.
2. Read the **DISC.pdf** attached to the Trello card for the background material.
3. Share your results with your manager.
