## Purpose

The Daily Huddle is the team's one time together each day. This SOP tells you when it happens, how it runs, and what you need to have ready so your share takes 60 seconds or less.

## When

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Days | Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (per the Trello card title) |
| Start | 8:20 AM, promptly |
| End | 8:30 AM (10 minutes total) |
| Timer | A designated person times each share |
| Time per person | 60 seconds maximum |

> **Note for the trainer:** the linked doc says "Daily 8:20 AM"; the Trello card title says M/T/TH/F. Confirm with the new coordinator whether Wednesday is skipped.

## Why we take it seriously

- We show up on time and are not late, because we respect each other's time.
- If a meeting you are in is running long, invite the person you are meeting with to join the Daily Huddle and explain that it is the most important meeting of the day. It is our one time together, and togetherness is a core value.
- Come prepared. We are professionals, not amateurs.
- We are here to celebrate the wins.

## The four rounds

Each person shares all four items, in this order, within 60 seconds.

### 1. Wins

Something significant you worked on in the last business day that moved the company forward. Ask yourself: did you make a connection? Did you hit a win from your monthly goals?

Examples from the doc: mind-mapped core values and a purpose statement; shared core values with the team; updated the list of potential investors.

### 2. Speed bumps

What is stopping you from getting work done, or what customers are having issues with. Why? What is holding you back?

Examples from the doc: juggling 18 projects plus caregiving; chasing Company X for late rent; Property Management being unresponsive.

### 3. Gratitude

Appreciation for someone on or off the team who did an extraordinary job or helped the company that day.

Examples from the doc: Kalen for showing me Miro; David Grant for sending the LLC agreement; Paige for the construction schedule.

### 4. WOD / Intention Word

Set your intention for the day in one word: how you will focus on your SMART goals. It is not the weather.

Examples from the doc: Inspired, Plowing, Optimistic. A word list is linked from the doc if you need ideas.

## Etiquette

1. Do not interrupt, and do not ask clarifying questions until everyone has shared.
2. Keep to 60 seconds. We want to hear from everyone.
3. After the call there is an optional parking lot to discuss speed bumps. If a speed bump is outside your expertise, you can drop off.

## How to prepare (before 8:20)

1. Write down one win from the last business day.
2. Write down one speed bump (or "none").
3. Pick one person to thank and why.
4. Pick your intention word.
5. Be on the call by 8:20 so it can start on time.

## Format at a glance

| Start | Timing | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8:20 to 8:30 | Organizer times each person (60 s) | Wins | Speed bump | Gratitude | Intention Word |
