## Purpose
One rule about Google Reviews that every Hospitality Coordinator must know before talking to members about our online presence.

## The rule

> **Warning:** We are **not allowed to ask anyone to leave a review.** This applies to members, guests, vendors and visitors, at both RED and SEA.

## What this means day to day

1. Never ask a member, tour guest or visitor to write a Google Review, even casually ("if you liked the tour, leave us a review").
2. Do not hand out cards, QR codes or links that request a review.
3. If someone offers unprompted to leave a review, you may thank them, but do not solicit or coach the wording.
4. If you are unsure whether something counts as "asking", treat it as asking and don't do it.
