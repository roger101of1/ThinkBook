## Purpose
How to offer the Meeting Owl (a 360-degree conference camera and speaker) to members, who to email when it is used, and the rules for getting it back.

## Redmond (RED)

1. The Meeting Owl is rarely used by members, but it is available to them **for free**. If you see a member having a meeting, let them know we have it.
2. KB will train you on how to set it up. Add your own setup notes to the Trello card once you have been trained.
3. Every time someone uses it, **notate the use by emailing Abby and KB**.

> **Note for the trainer:** the card says "Add notes here on how to set up once KB trains you." No setup steps have been written yet; add them here after the training.

## Seattle (SEA)

1. When the Meeting Owl is used, **email Hannah**.

## Return rules (both locations)

| Rule | What to do |
|---|---|
| Normal use | The member must **return the Owl by the end of the day, before you go home**. |
| Member asks to use it **after 4 pm** | Notate the after-4pm request in your email. Tell the member to **leave the Owl in the conference room they used** and to **secure the door by closing it**. |

> **Warning:** do not leave for the day with the Owl unaccounted for. Either it is back with you, or you have emailed that it was left in a closed conference room after an after-4pm request.
