## Purpose
How to work out, from anywhere, whether the Redmond (RED) building has lost power, Internet, or both, for example during a high-wind storm.

## The one rule

> **Warning:** follow **ALL six checks** below. No single check guarantees the answer. Several of the tools need both power and Internet, so one failing site can mean either outage. Only the combined picture tells you what is really down.

## The six checks

| # | Check | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PSE Outage Map** | [pse.com/en/outage/outage-map](https://www.pse.com/en/outage/outage-map) | Whether the building is inside a power-outage area. **Not 100% foolproof.** |
| 2 | **iVU** | [ivu.thinkspace.com](https://ivu.thinkspace.com) | iVU uses both power and Internet. If the site does not come up, it could be a loss of **either**. |
| 3 | **Arlo cameras** | [my.arlo.com](https://my.arlo.com) | The cameras use both power and Internet. No video could mean a loss of power **or** Internet. |
| 4 | **Ziply outage map** | [ziplyfiber.com](https://www.ziplyfiber.com) help center, then "internet outage" | May tell you whether there is an **Internet** outage in the area. |
| 5 | **Xfinity Outage Map** | [xfinity.com/support/statusmap](https://www.xfinity.com/support/statusmap) | Whether Xfinity reports an outage in the area. |
| 6 | **Brivo** | [access.brivo.com](https://access.brivo.com) | You can log in, but if you see communication errors connecting to the controllers, Brivo is down and there may be a **power** outage. |

## How to read the results

This is a reading guide based on what each check depends on (see the table above), not an extra rule from the doc.

- **PSE shows the building in an outage area** and iVU, Arlo and Brivo all fail: most likely a **power outage**.
- **PSE shows no outage** but iVU and Arlo fail and Ziply or Xfinity report a problem: most likely an **Internet outage**.
- Mixed or unclear results: remember that iVU and Arlo depend on both services, and that the PSE map is not foolproof. Report what you found on each check rather than guessing.
