## Purpose
Stamps.com is how you buy postage and print shipping labels for outgoing member mail. This page tells you where the logins are and what to cover with your trainer.

## Logins
| Location | Where the login lives |
|---|---|
| **RED** (Redmond) | Stamps.com login for Redmond is **in 1Password** |
| **SEA** (Seattle) | Stamps.com login for Seattle is **in 1Password** |

Each location has its own Stamps.com account. Make sure you are in the right one before printing postage, so the cost lands on the correct location.

## Training discussion
1. Log in to the account for your location from 1Password.
2. **Discuss creating labels for members**, for example for **Rick Miller**. Some members regularly need shipping labels created for them; agree with your trainer how those requests come in and how they are charged back.

> **Note for the trainer:** the card has an empty checklist and no written steps. Demonstrate creating a label end to end (address, weight, service, print) and add the steps to the card so they can be transcribed here.
