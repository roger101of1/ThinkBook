## Purpose

A complaint handled well creates a more loyal guest than no complaint at all. This SOP gives you a repeatable way to do that.

## The LEARN method

- **L — Listen.** Let the guest finish. Do not interrupt or explain yet.
- **E — Empathize.** *"I understand, and I'm sorry this happened."*
- **A — Apologize.** Once, sincerely, on behalf of the property — even if it wasn't your fault.
- **R — Resolve.** Offer a concrete fix and a time. Give options where you can.
- **N — Notify.** Log it, tell the Duty Manager if it meets the escalation criteria, and follow up.

## Recovery options you can offer without approval

- Complimentary item under **$20**.
- Late checkout to 1:00 PM.
- Room move within the same category.
- A hand-written apology note with an amenity.

## Escalate to the Duty Manager when

- The guest asks for a refund, or any compensation over **$50**.
- The complaint involves safety, security, discrimination, or harassment.
- The guest threatens a public review, legal action, or is visibly distressed.
- The same guest complains a **second time** during their stay.

## Never

- Argue, blame another department, or say "there's nothing I can do."
- Promise something you cannot personally guarantee.
- Discuss the complaint in front of other guests — move to a quiet area.

## Documentation

Every complaint is logged as a **Complaint** ticket with: what happened, what was offered, whether the guest was satisfied, and whether it was escalated. Complaint tickets are reviewed weekly.
