## Purpose
What you need to learn in Kube (the Yardi Kube portal, used for contracts and bookings) on Day 0, and where to send the questions you should not handle yourself.

## The three rules

| Situation | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Conference room bookings** | Learn how to make Conference Room Bookings in Kube. This is your job. |
| **Billing questions** | Refer them to **Abby**. |
| **Every other inquiry** | Refer them to **KB**. |

## Steps

1. On Day 0, have your trainer walk you through making a Conference Room Booking in Kube, then make one yourself.
2. When a member asks about billing (invoices, charges, payments), do not try to answer it in Kube. Hand it to Abby.
3. Anything else about Kube (contracts, accounts, changes) goes to KB.
