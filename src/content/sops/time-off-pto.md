## Purpose
How to request time off and how sick time works.

## Requesting time off

1. Submit the time off request in your **ADP app**.
2. **Also email Abby** so she can add it to the PTO calendar.
3. Give **at least 2 weeks notice**.

> **Note for the trainer:** The Employee Handbook adds more rules: PTO accrues but cannot be taken during the first 90 days; requests for time off between November 20 and December 31 must be submitted in writing before October 15; longest-serving employees get scheduling preference. See the Employee Handbook SOP.

## Sick time

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Accrual | You accrue **1 hour of sick time for every 40 hours worked** |
| What you can use it for | Doctor appointments, or when you are sick |
| Why | This is **Washington State law** |

## Quick checks
- Request in ADP **and** email Abby.
- Two weeks notice.
- Sick time: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, usable for doctor appointments or illness.
