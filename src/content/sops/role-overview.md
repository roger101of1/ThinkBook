## Purpose

The Hospitality Coordinator is the first and last person most guests interact with. Your job is to make every guest feel expected, welcomed, and taken care of — from the moment they arrive until they leave.

## Core responsibilities

1. **Guest arrival & check-in** — greet, verify, orient, and hand over keys.
2. **Guest requests** — log, fulfil, and close the loop on every request within the service-level targets.
3. **Issue recovery** — own complaints until they are resolved, and escalate when needed.
4. **Coordination** — keep Housekeeping, Maintenance, and the Front Desk in sync through the shared task board.
5. **Reporting** — file incident reports and end-of-shift notes before leaving.

## Who you work with

| Team | What you rely on them for | Primary channel |
|---|---|---|
| Housekeeping | Room readiness, turnover | Task board + radio |
| Maintenance | Repairs, safety fixes | Task board |
| Front Desk | Reservations, billing | In person / phone |
| Duty Manager | Escalations, approvals over $50 | Phone |

## Shift structure

- **Start of shift (first 15 min):** read the handover notes, check today's arrivals, walk the lobby.
- **During shift:** monitor the task board every 30 minutes; nothing sits "unassigned" for more than 15 minutes.
- **End of shift (last 15 min):** write handover notes, close or reassign every open task, file any incident reports.

## Key standards to remember

- Greet every guest within **10 seconds** of them entering your area.
- Every guest request gets a **ticket** — nothing lives only in your head.
- Never promise a compensation over **$50** without Duty Manager approval.
