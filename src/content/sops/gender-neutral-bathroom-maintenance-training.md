## Purpose

The Seattle (SEA) location has gender neutral bathrooms on Floor 1. Members need to know what is and is not acceptable there, and employees need to know what to do when there is a power outage or a leak in those bathrooms.

## Two training requirements (SEA only)

| Who | What they need to be trained on |
|---|---|
| **Members in SEA** | What is acceptable and what is not acceptable in the gender neutral bathrooms. |
| **Employees** | What happens if there is a **power outage** or a **leak** in the **1F (Floor 1) bathrooms**. |

## What you should do

1. Learn the acceptable-use rules for the SEA gender neutral bathrooms from your trainer, so you can explain them to members.
2. Learn the power outage and leak procedure for the 1F bathrooms. Remember that **Floor 1 toilets in Seattle are a property management item**, so a leak there means calling property management (see the SOP "Items Property Management Must Be Called to Fix" for the Seattle emergency numbers).

> **Note for the trainer:** the Trello card only states these two training requirements; it does not spell out the rules or the outage/leak procedure. Cover them in person and add them here.
