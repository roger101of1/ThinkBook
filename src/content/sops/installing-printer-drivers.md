## Purpose
What to do when a member needs the printer added to their PC or Mac, or the printer needs service. You do not install drivers yourself; you route the member to our printer vendor.

## Who does the work

We pay **Pacific Automation** to come out and service the printer, fix any issues, and help people add the printer to their PC or Mac.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Vendor | **Pacific Automation** (Service) |
| Service number | **206-576-0256** |
| Equipment ID to quote | **8C11830** |

## Steps

1. Direct the member to **call Pacific Automation Service at 206-576-0256**.
2. Give the member the **Equipment ID: 8C11830** so Pacific Automation can find our printer.
3. Pacific Automation can help the member **remotely**, or they can **come out and install in person**.
4. If the member does not want Pacific Automation to access their PC or Mac remotely **for confidentiality reasons**, tell the member to ask for an **in-person** visit instead.
