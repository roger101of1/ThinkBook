## Purpose

Anything that could lead to injury, loss, damage, or a claim must be documented the same shift it happens.

## What counts as an incident

- Any injury to a guest, visitor, or team member — however minor.
- Property damage (ours or a guest's).
- Theft, missing items, or suspected theft.
- Security concerns: unauthorized person, threats, intoxicated or disruptive guests.
- Near misses — something that *could* have caused injury (wet floor, loose railing).

## Steps

1. **Make it safe.** Deal with the immediate hazard or call for help first.
2. **Inform the Duty Manager** by phone — immediately for injuries and security incidents, before end of shift for everything else.
3. **Collect facts:** who, what, where, when, witnesses, and photos if appropriate. Facts only — no opinions about fault.
4. **File the Incident Report form** on the task board before you leave. Late reports are treated as incomplete.
5. **Do not** offer compensation or admit liability. Say: *"I'm sorry this happened. We'll take care of you and I'm documenting everything now."*

## Timelines

| Incident type | Notify Duty Manager | File report |
|---|---|---|
| Injury | Immediately | Same shift |
| Security | Immediately | Same shift |
| Damage / theft | Within 1 hour | Same shift |
| Near miss | End of shift | Same shift |

## After filing

The Duty Manager reviews every report within 24 hours. You may be asked follow-up questions — keep your own notes for a week.
