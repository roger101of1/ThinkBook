## Purpose

Know what to do before you need to. Read this fully, then locate the items below on your first shift: the fire panel, the nearest extinguishers, the AED, the first-aid kit, and the assembly point.

## Fire alarm

1. Do **not** assume it is a drill.
2. Call the Duty Manager and confirm whether the fire department has been called; if you cannot reach anyone within **1 minute**, call **911** yourself.
3. Take the **arrivals/in-house list** and go to the assembly point.
4. Direct guests to the nearest exit — never to elevators.
5. At the assembly point, help account for guests using the in-house list. Report anyone unaccounted for to the fire officer.
6. Do not re-enter until the fire officer or Duty Manager clears the building.

## Medical emergency

1. Call **911** first if the person is unconscious, not breathing, bleeding heavily, or has chest pain.
2. Then call the Duty Manager.
3. Send someone to meet the ambulance at the entrance.
4. Get the AED if needed — you are trained to use it; follow the voice prompts.
5. Stay with the person. Do not move them unless they are in danger.
6. File an Incident Report the same shift.

## Power outage

1. Emergency lighting comes on automatically. Check that it did.
2. Check the elevators for trapped guests; call the elevator emergency line if anyone is inside.
3. Reassure guests in the lobby; hand out flashlights from the emergency cabinet.
4. Duty Manager coordinates with the utility.

## Threat or violent person

- Do not confront. Keep distance, stay calm, and call **911**, then the Duty Manager.
- Move guests away from the area if you can do so safely.
- Note description, direction of travel, and any vehicle.
