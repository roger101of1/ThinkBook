## Purpose
How to log in to the HVAC control site for Redmond.

## Login

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| URL | http://IVU.Thinkspace.com |
| Username | Redmond |
| Password | **see 1Password** |

## Steps

1. Open http://IVU.Thinkspace.com (bookmarked on the laptop). It runs on the Comcast internet connection, so check the Xfinity outage map if it will not load.
2. Log in with username **Redmond** and the password from 1Password.
3. For what temperatures to set, see "HVAC Temperature for Redmond" in the Tech Info section of the Operational Directory.

> **Warning:** The Trello card itself shows the password in plain text. Do not copy it anywhere. Use 1Password, and ask your manager to remove the password from the card.
