## Purpose

ViewGlass is the automatic dynamic window tinting system in the Seattle (SEA) Lake Union Building. This SOP explains how the tint works, how to give a member access, how the member sets up the app, how zones are named, and how to manage accounts in the app. The card also links the Seattle Operational Directory, the index of all Seattle SOPs.

## How ViewGlass works

- ViewGlass is the automatic dynamic tinting system for the external windows throughout the Lake Union Building.
- Tint is controlled by **roof sensors plus a pre-set schedule** that picks the optimal tint per zone, so windows tint automatically through the day.
- You can **override the tint temporarily** with the ViewGlass app.
- **Permanent schedule changes** go to the ViewGlass rep, not through the app.

## Who has access

- Thinkspace staff have access to **all tint zones** and can adjust an office's tint for a member temporarily. This is most often done for **Office Divvy** members.
- Members are **not** automatically granted access. **Private Office and Dedicated Desk** members can request it.

## Granting a member access

1. Email the ViewGlass rep with the member's **name**, **email**, and the **zones needed**.
2. Rep: **Alex Palomarez**, alex.palomarez@view.com.
3. Use the zone map (a Drive file linked from the ViewGlass doc) to identify the right zones.

## Member app setup (the 5 steps from the welcome email)

1. Download View's **Dynamic Glass** app (blue icon) from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
2. In **Settings**, enter the IP address **50.251.196.13** (include the dots).
3. Wait for the initial server connection. The app can run in the background.
4. Tap the **Settings icon (bottom-right)**, then **Login**.
5. Login = the user's assigned login (lower case); password = the shared app password (lower case), **see 1Password**.

iPhone and Android user guides are linked from view.com (and from the Seattle Operational Directory).

> **Note for the trainer:** the ViewGlass doc says the app is on the Apple App Store and Google Play in the setup steps, but the welcome email template says it is only in the Apple Store. Confirm which is current before sending the welcome email.

## Zone naming

Example: **"2E Coworking 1B"**

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Floor |
| E | Direction. E windows face Lake Union. |
| Coworking | Room |
| 1 | Window set. 1 is closest to the stage; numbers ascend, and 4 is nearest the huddle rooms. |
| B | Bottom pane. **T** = Top. |

## Managing accounts in the app (advanced users)

1. Open the **USERS** tab at the bottom of the app.
2. Edit an existing account, or tap **+** to create a new one.
3. Fill in: **NAME** (what is displayed), **LOGIN** (the login name), **PASSWORD** (their password).
4. Keep the password consistent across accounts to make troubleshooting easier. Always use **LOWER CASE** for name, login and password.
5. Under **PERMISSIONS**, keep the role as **"Basic"**, then assign the **Zones** the member should control.
6. Always hit **Done / Save**.

> **Warning:** never write the shared app password into an email, Trello card or this SOP. It lives in 1Password only.
