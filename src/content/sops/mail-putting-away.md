## Purpose
Putting away member mail is learned by doing it alongside someone, then on your own. This page sets out the four-day ramp so you and your trainer both know when supervision ends.

## The training ramp
Work through the days in order. Each "day" is a working day on which mail is put away.

| Day | How you put away the mail |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | With supervision: a trainer works alongside you |
| Day 2 | With supervision: a trainer works alongside you |
| Day 3 | Without supervision: you put the mail away on your own |
| Day 4 onward | Without supervision, every day |

1. **Days 1 and 2:** put away the mail with your trainer watching and correcting. Ask about anything that does not have an obvious home (unknown names, former members, packages).
2. **Day 3:** put away the mail without supervision. Your trainer is available for questions but does not stand with you.
3. **Day 4 onward:** putting away the mail is fully yours, unsupervised, every day.

> **Note for the trainer:** the original card pins the ramp to specific June dates (June 7 and 8 supervised, June 9 unsupervised, June 10 onward unsupervised) for one particular hire. Treat those as "Day 1 to Day 4" of whichever week the new coordinator starts.
