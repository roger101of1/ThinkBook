## Purpose

Some building problems are the landlord's or property manager's responsibility, not ours. This SOP lists what to hand off at each location and who to call in a maintenance emergency. The lists differ between Seattle (SEA) and Redmond (RED), so check the right one.

## Seattle (SEA, 1700 Westlake Ave N, Lake Union Building)

Call property management for:

1. **Butterfly** (the callbox at the main entrance outside)
2. **Toilets on Floor 1**
3. **HVAC heating and cooling issues**
