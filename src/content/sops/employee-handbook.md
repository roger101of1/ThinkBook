## Employment practices and processes

### Equal employment policy
- Thinkspace is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, age, disability, marital status, sexual orientation, or any other legally protected status.
- Applies to all terms and conditions of employment: hiring, placement, promotion, termination, reduction in force, transfer, leaves of absence, compensation and training.
- Managers and supervisors are responsible for preventing discrimination, but success depends on the unbiased, respectful actions of all employees.

### Non-discrimination and anti-harassment policy
- Everyone has the right to a non-discriminatory, non-intimidating workplace. Harassment based on race, religion, color, sex, age, marital status, sexual orientation and/or any physical, mental or sensory disability is a serious violation. **Discrimination and harassment will not be condoned or tolerated.**
- Harassment can include slurs, comments, jokes, innuendoes, unwelcome compliments, cartoons, visual displays, e-mails, pranks, and other verbal, visual or physical conduct that (1) creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive working environment, (2) unreasonably interferes with work performance, or (3) unreasonably affects employment opportunities.
- **Sexual harassment** is that conduct when it is sexual in nature or gender-based. It exists when submission is a term or condition of employment, when submission or rejection is used as the basis of an employment decision, or when the conduct unreasonably interferes with job performance or creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive environment. It will not be condoned or tolerated.
- All employees, whatever their title, are prohibited from discriminating, harassing, or **retaliating** against anyone who makes a good-faith complaint. Violators face discipline up to and including termination.
- **Duty to report:** report any discrimination or harassment problem (involving managers, employees, or others you encounter at work) to the **Human Resources Department**. Complaints are promptly investigated and corrective action taken if appropriate.
- Retaliation against anyone who brings a complaint or speaks as a witness is prohibited.

### Disability accommodation
- Thinkspace complies with state and federal disability discrimination laws and will not discriminate against a qualified employee or applicant because of a disability or perceived disability, as long as the person can perform the essential functions of the job with or without reasonable accommodation.
- Reasonable accommodation is provided to a qualified individual who has made the company aware of the disability, provided it is not an undue hardship on the company.
- If you need an accommodation, contact the Human Resources Department.

### Job responsibilities
- Everyone pitches in. Many tasks are shared; we pride ourselves on teamwork and proactively helping each other so nobody feels they do all the work.
- Your supervisor will explain your job description. It may change as the business changes; your supervisor will inform you.

### Hiring criteria
Selection is based on qualifications, skills, training and experience, past performance, personality, temperament, ability to work with current staff, overall ability to perform the work, and willingness to display a loyal and cooperative attitude.

### Background screening
A signed consent form is attached to the employment application. Applicants unwilling to have their background checked will not be considered.

### Immigration compliance
- Thinkspace employs only US citizens and aliens authorized to work in the US and does not unlawfully discriminate on citizenship or national origin.
- Under the Immigration Reform and Control Act of 1986, every new employee must complete **Form I-9** and present documentation of identity and employment eligibility as a condition of employment.
- Rehired former employees must also complete it if they have not completed an I-9 with the company within the past **three years**, or if their previous I-9 is no longer retained or valid.
- You may raise questions or complaints about immigration compliance without fear of reprisal.

### Alcohol and drug policy
- Possessing, using, or being under the influence of alcohol on the job or on company property is prohibited. Possessing, using or trafficking in illegally used drugs on the job or on company property is prohibited.
- Being under the influence on company property or while representing Thinkspace, or using, selling or possessing alcohol or illegal drugs on company time or property, leads to discipline up to and including termination. Determination and enforcement are at the company's discretion.
- **Pre-employment testing:** Thinkspace may require a pre-employment drug screening (urine specimen sent to an authorized lab). Satisfactory results must be returned to HR before the person may begin work.
- **Post-employment testing:** a manager may order testing on "reasonable suspicion" that an employee is under the influence. Reasonable suspicion may be based on unusual behavior such as decreased productivity, abnormally high accident rates, repeated tardiness or absence, aggressive or violent behavior, emotional outbursts or moodiness, sensory or motor skill malfunctions, or behavior that poses a threat to self or others. If an employee is observed to be impaired, the manager initiates testing and then arranges for someone to take the employee home.
- **Random testing:** Thinkspace may periodically test employees at random, without reasonable suspicion.
- **Refusing** a reasonable-suspicion or random test is noted, is a violation of policy, and may result in discipline including termination.

### HIPAA
Since April 14, 2003, essentially all employees are required to adhere to the HIPAA Privacy Rule. Training and updates will be provided if necessary. Every employee must remain in compliance with HIPAA at all times.

### Introductory period
- The **first 90 days** of employment are an introductory period. Your manager evaluates your performance and ability to meet company standards.
- Near the end, your performance is formally evaluated in a review with your manager.
- If performance is marginal or unsatisfactory, the company may end employment **or extend the introductory period for up to another 90 days**.
- Employment remains at-will during and after the introductory period.

### Rehires
- Former employees who voluntarily separated and reapply are considered on the same basis as any other applicant.
- After a voluntary resignation, layoff, or similar separation, you **lose prior service credit if your break in service is more than 90 days**.

### Employment references
- All requests for employment and/or credit references must be forwarded to the **Human Resources Department**.
- Unless the employee gives written authorization, or a court order or government agency requires otherwise, Thinkspace discloses only **job title, verification of employment dates, and base compensation**.

### Employment of relatives
- Thinkspace does not discriminate on marital status. Employment of qualified spouses, domestic partners and relatives of current associates is permitted unless it would create an actual or reasonably foreseeable conflict of interest, for example one spouse supervising, appointing, removing, disciplining or auditing the work of the other.
- "Relatives" are spouses, children, parents, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, first cousins and corresponding in-laws and "step" relations.
- Applies to employees who marry while employed. No employee is discharged because of marrying another employee unless the conflict cannot be resolved by transferring one or both.
- Thinkspace prohibits employing relatives and domestic partners of policy-level officers of significant customers, competitors, regulatory agencies and others we deal with, to avoid improper influence and protect confidential information.

### Employment records
- Personnel files are kept at Thinkspace Corporate Offices. **Notify your manager or HR immediately of any change in personal status** (payroll, insurance and benefits depend on accurate information).
- Files are confidential with limited access. You may review your own personnel file; ask your manager or HR.
- Managers and supervisors may not provide written reference letters without specific HR approval.

### Licensure and registration
- If your position requires a license, registration or certification, you must keep it current and keep HR and your manager up to date on status changes.
- Employees with expired required licenses may not perform licensed work; they will do non-licensed work if available, or not work until the license is reinstated.

### Categories of employment

| Category | Definition | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract employees** | Independent contractors at a set hourly or project rate. Hours are not guaranteed. | Not entitled to leaves of absence, PTO, or group insurance. |
| **Part-time employees** | Work **less than 32 hours a week** with a set schedule. | Not eligible for PTO and benefits unless the benefit plan document or employment contract says otherwise. |
| **Full-time employees** | Non-exempt employees regularly scheduled **at least 32 hours per week**, and salaried exempt employees. | Generally eligible for the benefits in the handbook, subject to eligibility rules. |

- **Exempt** employees (qualified executive, administrative or professional) are paid on a salary basis and do not receive overtime pay. **Non-exempt** employees are paid hourly for actual hours worked and receive overtime pay. Check with your manager or HR if unsure of your status.

## Job opportunities

### Recruitment, transfers and promotions
- Thinkspace encourages promotion from within, but internal and external candidates are generally considered for most management openings. The best qualified candidate is selected; **if two are equally qualified, the internal candidate is preferred**.
- Openings are announced through the job posting process. You may apply if (1) your performance is satisfactory, (2) you have been in your current job **at least 90 days**, and (3) you meet the minimum qualifications.
- Apply by submitting a request to the hiring manager and/or HR.

### Compensation
- Pay is affected by: the responsibility of the role (based on experience and education), level of performance (outstanding, not mediocre, is rewarded), and the company's ability to pay while remaining competitive.
- **Salary increases are based strictly on merit** and the company's assessment of its resources. There are **no cost-of-living, seniority or other automatic increases**.

### Performance reviews
- Formal annual performance review for all full-time employees at least once a year, on an HR-determined schedule, at the same time as comparable positions or the whole location. It is a development activity. Additional reviews may happen during the year.
- After a **promotion**, you receive a review on or around the **90th day** in the new position.

### Pay periods
- **Bi-weekly** pay period. **Paychecks are issued on the 5th and 20th of the month.**
- All non-exempt employees must enter their actual hours worked on the company time clock.

### Time keeping requirements
- All non-exempt employees track hours through the company time clock system (ADP). Hours must accurately reflect exact time worked.
- **Time clock updates are due to HR before 8:00am on the Monday before payday.**
- Enter time in **real time**, neither in advance nor delayed.
- Entering time for another associate, or falsifying hours, is forbidden and leads to discipline up to and including termination.

### Overtime for non-exempt employees
- Overtime, when requested by management, is usually voluntary; management plans to keep it to a minimum. Sometimes overtime is mandatory and non-exempt employees are expected to comply.
- Overtime is paid per state and federal law.
- **All overtime must have prior approval from management.**
- **PTO hours do not count as hours worked** when calculating overtime.

### Payroll deductions
Deductions from gross pay cover income taxes, Social Security, insurance premiums, etc. Thinkspace has no choice but to make them. Take-home pay = total earnings minus deductions.

### Garnishments
A garnishment is a legal action by a creditor to obtain funds held by a third party for a debtor. The Federal Consumer Credit Protection Act (FCCPA) gives certain protections. Garnishments appear on your pay stub under **"Miscellaneous 1"**. If Thinkspace receives a garnishment, wage attachment or IRS levy, HR informs the employee.

### Expense reimbursement
- Reasonable business expenses (mileage, parking, supplies, meals, lodging, etc.) are reimbursed.
- Expense forms must be completed, **approved by your manager**, and turned in **with receipts** to the Finance Department and your directing manager **by the end of the month in which the expense is incurred**.
- Reimbursements are added to the next payroll.
- If you drive your personal vehicle on Thinkspace business and are reimbursed for mileage, you are **not covered by the company's insurance**; obtain private insurance.

### Termination
- If you resign, give **as much notice as possible** so a replacement can be hired and trained.
- **Thinkspace does not pay out unused PTO.**
- Termination pay is reduced by required legal deductions, the employee portion of the monthly group insurance plan, and deductions for any company property not returned.
- The final paycheck is normally available on the **next payroll following the termination date**, through normal channels; alternative arrangements (mailed, at exit interview) may be made.

This is a general description only. Thinkspace does not promise any benefit will continue and may terminate or modify benefits at any time, with or without notice.

### Government mandated benefits

If you need medical attention, tell the treating physician the injury is job-related; the doctor provides the State Fund Report of Industrial Injury or Occupational Disease (the official claim form). ### Health insurance
- Full-time/part-time employees working **a minimum of 32 to 40 hours a week** are eligible for group health insurance.
- Eligible employees may enroll in medical, dental, vision and life benefits **after 30 days of continuous employment**. Benefits begin on the **first day of the month following 30 continuous days** of employment.
- Full coverage costs are for employees only; dependents may be added but the employee pays their coverage amount by payroll deduction.
- If you take a personal leave of absence you are responsible for paying your monthly premium (see leave guidelines).
- You have **until the end of the first 30 days** of continuous employment to make benefit elections. Elections are fixed for the plan year unless you have a **"Qualifying Change of Life Event"**, in which case you may change coverage **within 30 days of the event** (contact HR).
- When employment ends, coverage for you and dependents continues **until the last day of that month**; the last payroll accounts for any final premiums owed.

## Leaves of absence

- Leaves are subject to managerial approval. Advise the company **at least thirty days in advance** when possible, and schedule leaves to avoid unduly disrupting operations. Eligibility conditions apply; leave may be denied or shortened.
- While on family and medical leave you must use all accrued paid leave. **PTO does not accrue** during the portion of leave that is company-paid.
- You may continue health coverage at the same level and conditions as if you had not taken leave. Arrange payment of your portion of premiums with HR before leave. **Premium payments more than 30 days late result in discontinued health coverage.**

### Compassionate leave
- Arrangements for paid compassionate leave or unpaid leave for death or serious illness must be made in advance with the administrator.
- Death in the immediate family: **five work days off with pay**. Immediate family = spouse, parents, parents of spouse, stepparents, legal guardian, children, stepchildren, siblings, step-siblings, siblings-in-law, grandparents, grandparents-in-law, and grandchildren.
- If the death occurs while you are on scheduled vacation, the vacation converts to compassionate leave for the period you would have been granted.
- With manager approval you may use PTO for additional time, and may be allowed unpaid time off to attend the funeral of other persons.

### Jury and witness duty
- Time off is allowed to serve on a jury or as a witness (when you are not a litigant). Your job is protected.
- Thinkspace **supplements jury pay to equal your normal pay for up to five (5) business days**. You keep any transportation or lodging allowances.
- Substantiate the time with an official court document showing dates served and the jury/witness pay amount.

### Military service
- Annual reserve training or other short-term active duty can be taken as PTO or unpaid military leave. Reservists return to their jobs per federal and state law.
- Report military obligations to your manager as early as possible.
- Extended tours of duty qualify for an extended military leave of absence; company insurance benefits end on the last day of the month in which the extended leave begins. Reemployment rights follow state and federal law.

### Family and medical leave of absence (WA PFML)
- Due to its size, Thinkspace qualifies for **Washington Paid Family and Medical Leave (WA PFML)**.
- **Eligibility:** you must have worked **at least 820 hours**.
- **Amount:** up to **12 work weeks** of paid leave, paid by the State of Washington.
- **Reasons:** to care for your child after birth or placement for adoption or foster care; to care for a family member with a serious health condition; for your own serious health condition that makes you unable to perform the essential functions of your job.
- **Serious health condition** includes: inpatient care; incapacity of more than three consecutive calendar days with multiple or continuing treatment by a health care provider; incapacity due to pregnancy or prenatal care; a chronic condition requiring periodic treatment with episodic incapacity (e.g. diabetes, epilepsy, asthma); a permanent or long-term condition requiring continuing supervision; or absence for multiple treatments (restorative surgery after an accident, or a condition that would cause more than three days of incapacity without treatment). **Incapacity** = inability to work, attend school, or perform regular daily activities.
- **Applying:** give **at least 30 days notice** if the leave is foreseeable (expected birth, placement, planned treatment). If not practical, give notice as soon as possible. State the purpose and anticipated dates.
- **Documentation:** medical certification on Thinkspace's form (Leave Request Form from HR). For foreseeable leave, submit **no later than 15 days before** leave starts. For emergency leave, submit **within 15 days** of the request. Late notice or certification can delay or deny leave.
- **Intermittent leave** (shorter blocks or reduced schedule) is allowed if medically necessary for a serious health condition; for birth/placement it requires Thinkspace's approval.
- **Employer-designated leave:** Thinkspace may designate any absence due to a serious health condition as WA PFML leave, with written notice.
- **Reinstatement:** Thinkspace may require a fitness-for-duty certification (form from HR) before you return. Reinstatement can be delayed or denied for failing to provide a requested certification, failing to return on the designated date, or obtaining leave fraudulently. **Failure to return at the end of leave is considered a resignation** unless an extension is applied for in writing and granted before the leave ends, supported by medical certification.
- **Status reports:** employees on extended leave must provide periodic status reports and may need recertification.
- **Health insurance during leave:** Thinkspace pays its portion through the end of the month in which leave begins; after that you pay the full premium at the group rate. During concurrent family and medical leave Thinkspace pays its portion. Other benefits (e.g. life insurance) continue during any paid portion of leave (while you use accrued PTO); during unpaid leave you pay the full premium.
- Applicable state and federal law may supersede this policy.

## Associate conduct and behavior

### Safety
Safety is everyone's responsibility. You must:
1. Perform job duties safely, following safe practices.
2. Report any accident to a supervisor immediately.
3. Report unsafe conditions, equipment or practices.
4. Keep hallways and working areas clear of hazards.
5. Use proper lifting procedures at all times.

### Accidents
- Report **all accidents, no matter how minor**, as soon as possible to your supervisor or manager.
- If medical treatment is required, get the proper paperwork from your manager or HR to take to the doctor. A **doctor's authorization is required before returning to work**. The injured employee completes an **Accident Report** with a manager.

### Auto accidents
- Any auto accident while on company business (personal, company or rented car), whether or not you are injured, must be **reported immediately to a manager**, with a copy of any accident report.
- Notify your own insurance carrier if it was your personal car.

### Search process
- To safeguard employees and prevent drugs, Thinkspace may question anyone entering or leaving the premises and inspect packages, parcels, purses, bags or other possessions.
- Thinkspace may search desks, offices, lockers and other assigned spaces and their contents at any time. All offices, desks, files and lockers are Thinkspace property.
- Thinkspace is not responsible for personal items lost, damaged, stolen or destroyed on the premises.

### Workplace violence
Strictly prohibited:
- Threatening, intimidating, coercive, abusive, harassing or violent verbal, written or physical behavior (or the suggestion of it) toward others, including co-workers.
- Possessing firearms, explosives or other weapons anywhere on company property (including company vehicles and private vehicles on company property) or while conducting Thinkspace business.
- Willfully destroying company property or the property of others.
- Fighting on company premises, while conducting company business, or over work-related issues.

Report threats or acts of violence to your manager or HR. No adverse action is taken against good-faith reports. Substantiated violations lead to corrective action up to and including termination.

### Electronic communications policy
- Internet and e-mail are provided for the benefit of the company, to help you do your job. Every employee must maintain the company's public image and use them productively.
- **You are personally accountable for actions performed under your passwords.**
- **Software must be approved before installation**, must be fully licensed, and must be for business purposes.
- **Acceptable use:** you represent the company; use must be effective, ethical and lawful. Not for amusement or excessive personal use. Websites should be accessed primarily for business information related to your job; e-mail primarily for business contacts. **Internet relay chat rooms, channels and instant messaging may not be used.**
- **Unacceptable use:** no solicitation of non-company business, personal gain, or advancing individual views. Use must not disrupt the company or impair others' use of resources.
- You are responsible for the content of all text, audio or images you send or receive. All messages must have your name attached; no assumed names; do not obscure the origin of a message. Do not store illegal, inappropriate or indecent files. **Misdirected e-mails must be returned to the sender without disclosing the contents to anyone else.**
- No abusive, profane, obscene or offensive language. Harassment of any kind is prohibited. No derogatory or inflammatory remarks about race, gender, religion, national origin, sexual orientation, age, etc.

### Copyright issues
- Do not copy, edit or retransmit third-party copyrighted material electronically unless Thinkspace has a license. Download copyrighted material only per the host site's posted procedures or with express permission of Thinkspace's legal counsel.
- Do not commit Thinkspace to paying any fee to download material. If in doubt, contact the Information Systems Administrator. Violations may lead to company discipline or legal action by the copyright owner.
- To share something from a website with a co-worker or third party, **send the website address** rather than downloading and relaying the content.

### Software
Downloading software from the Internet, or uploading your own software, is prohibited unless expressly approved by the Systems Administrator (to prevent viruses).

### Security
- All messages created, sent or retrieved over company systems are Thinkspace property. The company may intercept, monitor, copy, review and download any communications or files, and can monitor attempts to access inappropriate websites (pornographic, file sharing, broadcast sites, etc.).
- **No expectation of privacy.** Never create or send a message unless you would be comfortable having anyone inside or outside the company read it.
- Do not send sensitive materials or "confidential information" over the Internet or e-mail. Messages may be disclosed to law enforcement or third parties without consent.
- Notify management of unsolicited, offensive materials received. Violations lead to discipline up to and including discharge.

### Attendance and absenteeism
Regular attendance is essential; teamwork is at the heart of the business. Unscheduled absences create hardship, disrupt business, add load to co-workers, and often cost the company to hire fill-in labor. The policy exists to monitor and control absenteeism fairly.

### General attendance guidelines
- If you cannot report to work, or will be **late by more than five minutes** beyond your expected start time, you must **personally call your supervisor** as far in advance of your start time as possible.
- Failure to call or notify may result in discipline up to and including termination.
- **No call, no show may be treated as job abandonment and employment will be terminated.**

### Smoking
Thinkspace has a smoke-free policy. **Smoking is prohibited in all Thinkspace offices.**

### Communication with the media and other outside representatives
- Thinkspace speaks with "one voice". Employees are **prohibited from issuing statements to the media** (television, radio, magazine or newspaper) about Thinkspace business unless expressly authorized.
- If a media representative approaches you, **refer the question to the CEO**.

### Conflicts of interest
- Avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the company's, or might compromise the company's integrity and reputation. A conflict occurs when you use your position for personal benefit through an investment, association or business relationship that interferes with independent judgment in Thinkspace's best interests.
- **Full-time employees should check with their manager before accepting a second job** (self-employed or otherwise).

### Grievance procedure
Thinkspace has an open door policy, but asks that differences be handled in this order (available to current and terminated employees):
1. Discuss the issue first with the individuals directly involved.
2. If unsuccessful, discuss it with your manager.
3. If you are uncomfortable with the manager, unsatisfied with the result, or get no response in a reasonable time, go to the next management level (e.g. regional administrator).
4. If still unresolved in a reasonable time, discuss it with the **President of Thinkspace**.
5. At any time you may contact the **Human Resources Department** for help.

There will be **no retaliation** against any employee following this procedure.

### Suggestions
Ideas to improve productivity, the environment, safety and related matters are always welcome. Send suggestions to your manager, HR, or the President of Thinkspace.
