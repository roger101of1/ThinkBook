## Purpose
The list of hands-on skills your trainer must cover with you. Use it as a checklist and ask for any item you have not been shown.

## Training checklist

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setting up printer codes | How to give a member a code on the Konica printer. |
| 2 | Resetting a locked-out code | What to do when a member's code is locked out. |
| 3 | Meeting Owl | The conference room camera/speaker device (see Conference Room Amenities). |
| 4 | Installing the printer on a PC or Mac | **Do not troubleshoot this yourself: have the member call service at Konica.** |
| 5 | Emergency procedures | What to do in an emergency on site. |
| 6 | Power and Internet troubleshooting | First steps when power or internet goes down (the Operational Directory has "What to do if the power goes out" and "Internet Troubleshooting"). |

## How to use this list

1. Go through each item with your trainer during Day 0 and the first week.
2. Tick each one off only once you have done it yourself, not just watched.
3. For printer installation on a member's PC or Mac, the answer is always the same: refer them to Konica service.
