## Purpose
Who may park where at Redmond, what to do when the lots fill up, what extra parking costs, and what you must record for every member.

## RED (Redmond)

### The rules
1. Parking on the **top lot** and in the **underground parking** is **by Permit Only**, except in the **Visitor Parking** spaces. The top lot is **not a free for all**.
2. **If the Visitor Parking area is full**, clients of members can park **anywhere on top**.
3. **If the top lot is full**, we can **open the gate** and let members' clients park **underneath the building**.
4. Members are **not allowed to store their vehicles inside the parking garage**.

### When a member needs more parking
| Situation | What to offer | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Member has more employees than parking passes | A parking pass from **the neighbor next door** | **$85/month** per pass |
| Member wants a dedicated space | Rent a **Thinkspace tandem parking space**; it **must be assigned** to a specific member | **$100/month** |

### What you must record
- **Assign a parking pass to every single member.**
- **Log every member's license plate number.**

> **Warning:** A member without an assigned pass or a logged plate is a member whose car you cannot identify when the lot is full or a car is blocking someone. Do this at move-in, every time.
