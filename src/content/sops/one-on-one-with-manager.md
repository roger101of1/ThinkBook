## Purpose

You will have scheduled check-ins with your manager at your 30, 60 and 90 day marks. This SOP tells you what to expect.

## What happens

1. Your manager has already added calendar invites for your check-ins at **30, 60 and 90 days**.
2. Accept the invites when you see them so they stay on your calendar.
3. Prepare for each one using the 1:1 Trello format (see the SOP "Meeting Format for 1:1's"): add agenda items under the dated Agenda list before the meeting.
