## Purpose
Incoming mail for Virtual Office and office members is tracked in two systems. This page tells you which tools exist, where to log in, and how you get access.

## The two mail systems
| System | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **c3scan** | The admin dashboard used to manage scanned and logged member mail | [https://c3scan.thinkspace.com/admin/login?redirect=%2Fadmin%2Fdashboard](https://c3scan.thinkspace.com/admin/login?redirect=%2Fadmin%2Fdashboard) |
| **Piloto (PilotoMail)** | The mail platform Virtual Office members use to view, forward, scan, or shred their mail; you work it from the staff side | Access is by invitation link |

## Getting access
1. Open the c3scan admin login (link above) and sign in with your staff login. Credentials are **in 1Password**; never write them down elsewhere.
2. Your **Piloto** access arrives as an invitation link. On the card, the Piloto link was sent to **Peri@thinkspace.com**; if you have not received yours, ask your manager to send the invite to your thinkspace.com address.
3. Once you are in both systems, move on to the "Mail - Putting away" SOP for the hands-on training ramp.

> **Note for the trainer:** the card contains only the c3scan link and the note that the Piloto link was sent to Peri. Walk the trainee through the physical sorting system (files, slots, labels) in person, since it is not written down here yet.
