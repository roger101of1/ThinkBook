## Purpose
What a normal front desk day looks like for a Location Coordinator, from clock-in at 7:45am through the Daily Huddle, meeting room resets, mail work and writing up tomorrow's huddle notes.

## The daily rhythm (Front Desk)

| Time / cadence | Task | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7:45am | Log in via the website | Clock in on ADP through the website (or the ADP cell phone app). See the ADP Time Tracking SOP. |
| Start and end of day | Opening / Closing checklist | Takes 10 to 15 minutes. Follow the Daily Checklist doc (link below). |
| 8:20am | Daily Huddle | 8:20 to 8:30, starts promptly. Format below. |
| Throughout the day | Log breaks and lunch in ADP | Use the ADP app on your cell phone or the website. |
| Daily | Reset meeting rooms | Return every conference room to its ready state after use. |
| Daily, 60 min | Mail sorting | Block about an hour. |
| Thursday, 60 to 90 min | Mail forwarding | Weekly, on Thursdays. |
| End of day | Write up Daily Huddle notes for the next day | Prepare your Wins, Speed Bumps, Gratitude and Word of the Day so you arrive at the 8:20am huddle ready. |

## Step by step

1. **7:45am: clock in.** Log in via the website (ADP).
2. **Run the Opening checklist** (10 to 15 minutes). The Daily Checklist doc is linked below.
   > **Note for the trainer:** this document needs access granted to roger@thinkspace.com before it can be transcribed.
3. **8:20am: join the Daily Huddle** (format below). Bring the notes you wrote the day before.
4. **Log your breaks and lunch in ADP** via cell phone or website as they happen.
5. **Reset meeting rooms** so each one is clean and ready for the next booking.
6. **Sort the mail** (about 60 minutes).
7. **Thursdays: forward mail** (60 to 90 minutes).
8. **Before you leave:** run the Closing checklist (10 to 15 minutes) and write up your Daily Huddle notes for the next day: Wins, Speed Bumps, Gratitude and Word of the Day.

## Daily Huddle format (from the linked doc)

**Start time:** daily at 8:20am (8:20 to 8:30). It starts promptly.

**Why we take it seriously:**
- We show up on time and are not late because we respect each other's time.
- If a meeting with someone else is running long, invite that person to the Daily Huddle and explain that it is the most important meeting of the day: our one time together. Togetherness is a core value.
- Each person's share is limited to **60 seconds**. We want to hear from everyone.
- Come prepared. We are professionals, not amateurs.
- A designated person is the timer.
- We do not interrupt or ask clarifying questions until everyone has shared.
- Optional "parking lot" after the call to discuss speed bumps. If a speed bump is outside your expertise you can drop off.
- We are here to celebrate the wins.

**The four shares, in order:**

| # | Share | What to say | Examples from the doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wins** | Something significant you worked on in the last business day that moved the company forward. Did you make a connection? A win from your monthly goals? | Mind-mapped core values and a purpose statement; shared core values with the team; updated list of potential investors. |
| 2 | **Speed bumps** | What is stopping you from getting work done, or customers having issues. Why? What is holding you back? | Juggling 18 projects plus caregiving; chasing Company X for late rent; Property Management unresponsive. |
| 3 | **Gratitude** | Appreciation for someone on or off the team who did an extraordinary job or helped the company that day. | Kalen for showing me Miro; David Grant for sending the LLC agreement; Paige for the construction schedule. |
| 4 | **WOD / Intention Word** | Set your intention for the day in one word: how you will focus on your SMART goals. Not the weather. | Inspired, Plowing, Optimistic. A word list is linked from the huddle doc. |

Format summary: Start 8:20 to 8:30; the organizer times each person (60 seconds); 1 Wins, 2 Speed bump, 3 Gratitude, 4 Intention Word.

## Quick checks
- Clocked in on ADP at 7:45am?
- Opening checklist done (10 to 15 min)?
- At the huddle by 8:20am with Wins, Speed Bumps, Gratitude and Word of the Day ready, each under 60 seconds?
- Breaks and lunch logged in ADP?
- Meeting rooms reset, mail sorted (60 min), Thursday mail forwarding (60 to 90 min)?
- Tomorrow's huddle notes written before you leave?
