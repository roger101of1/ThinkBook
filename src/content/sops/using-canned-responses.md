## Purpose
Most member and prospect emails you send are variations on a handful of situations: a payment failed, someone wants Virtual Office pricing, a tour needs confirming, a new member needs building-entry instructions. Missive holds a template for each one. This SOP is the map of that library: the Missive name of every template, when to reach for it, and the facts, prices and steps it contains, so you can check a template is still correct before you send it.

## How to use a template
1. In Missive, open the conversation and insert the template by its name (the names in square brackets below are the exact names in Missive).
2. Fill in every placeholder in brackets, for example `[COMPANY]`, `[MONTH DUE]`, `[CURRENT MONTH DUE]`, and the member's name.
3. Read the whole message before sending. Check the location (RED vs SEA), the prices, and the phone number match the person you are writing to.
4. Never paste a member's username or password into an email from memory or from this page. The original "Welcome Email" template carried login lines; those credentials are **redacted** and live only in the member's Deskworks record. Do not reproduce credentials anywhere.

> **Warning:** Some templates are location-specific. Sending a RED template with the Redmond address and 425 phone number to a Seattle prospect (or vice versa) is a common mistake. Confirm the location first.

## Piloto compliance: acceptable IDs (from the "Piloto Compliance New Member" template)
The member must upload **two valid IDs**, and at least one must be a **government photo ID**. Expired documents are not accepted, and the same document cannot count twice.
