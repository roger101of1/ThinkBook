## Purpose
The office is the product members pay for. This page covers the standing expectation that it always looks clean and orderly, and who to ask when you need supplies.

## The standard
1. Always keep the office looking clean and orderly. Reset spaces as you pass through them so the next person finds them ready.
2. Treat this as a continuous responsibility, not a once-a-day task.

## When you need supplies
- If you need any **office supplies** or **cleaning supplies**, let **KB** know and she can order them.
- If you want a **privacy screen protector** (a filter that blocks anyone else from seeing your screen at the front desk), ask **KB** for that too; she orders those as well.
