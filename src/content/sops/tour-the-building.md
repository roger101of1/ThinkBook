## Purpose
The places your trainer must show you on your Day 1 building tour. You will later give tours to prospects, so learn where everything is.

## Tour checklist

| # | Stop | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garage + Thinkspace parking | Where members and guests park; parking rules and violations are part of the front desk job. |
| 2 | Both stairwells | Emergency routes and daily access between floors. |
| 3 | Shower | A member amenity you will be asked about. |
| 4 | All 3 floors | Know the layout of every floor (floor plans are in the Operational Directory). |
| 5 | Mail room | Where daily mail sorting (60 min) and Thursday mail forwarding happen. |
| 6 | All conference rooms | You book, reset and support these every day. |
| 7 | Team room | The staff space. |
| 8 | Tour office | The office used to show prospects. |
| 9 | Server room | Network equipment; know where it is for internet troubleshooting. |
| 10 | Copy room | Printer / copier (Konica) and printer codes. |

## Steps

1. Walk every stop above with your trainer.
2. Note which doors need a key, Brivo pass or code (codes are in 1Password or the Door Codes sheet only, never in your notes).
3. Ask which rooms members can use and which are staff only.

> **Note:** The list mentions "all 3 floors" and a garage, which matches the Redmond (RED) building. Ask your trainer for the equivalent SEA tour if you will work at Seattle.
