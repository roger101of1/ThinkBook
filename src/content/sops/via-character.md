## Purpose
Take the VIA Character Strengths survey as part of your personal development during onboarding.

## What it is

The VIA Character Strengths survey (VIA Institute on Character) is a free online questionnaire that ranks your 24 character strengths, such as curiosity, kindness, teamwork and humor, from strongest to weakest. It is used here so you and your manager can talk about how your natural strengths show up in the Hospitality Coordinator role.

## Steps

1. Go to the registration page linked below and **create a free account**.
2. Complete the survey (it takes roughly 10 to 15 minutes).
3. Save or download your results and share the summary with your manager.

> **Note for the trainer:** the card only contains the registration link. Confirm who the results should be shared with and whether there is a deadline.
