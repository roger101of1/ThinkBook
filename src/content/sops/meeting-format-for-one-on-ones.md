## Purpose

Every 1:1 with your manager is organized in Trello so nothing gets lost between meetings. This SOP explains the board structure and the date format.

## How 1:1s are organized

Every 1:1 is organized in Trello. The board has four lists:

| List | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Agenda** | Items to discuss. Each agenda card is named with the meeting date in the format **2025-mm-dd** so the list stays organized and chronological. |
| **Active** | Items that are being worked on right now. |
| **Completed** | Items that are done. |
| **Parking Lot** | Items to come back to later. |

## Steps before each 1:1

1. Open the 1:1 Trello board.
2. Add a card to **Agenda** dated in the **2025-mm-dd** format (year first, then month, then day, for example 2025-09-04). This keeps agendas in chronological order.
3. List what you want to cover on that card.
4. During the meeting, move items to **Active** as you start working on them, to **Completed** when done, or to **Parking Lot** if they should wait.
