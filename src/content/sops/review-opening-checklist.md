## Purpose

Review the Opening and Closing Checklist so you know every step of opening and closing the space each day. The checklist itself lives in the linked document.

## What the document is for

The "Opening and Closing Checklist" (also referred to as "Normal Day Schedule - Location Coordinator") is the daily run sheet for the front desk: what to do when you open in the morning and what to do when you close at the end of the day.

## Steps

1. Open the linked checklist and read it end to end.
2. Walk through the opening steps with your manager or trainer on your first opening shift.
3. Do the same for the closing steps on your first closing shift.
