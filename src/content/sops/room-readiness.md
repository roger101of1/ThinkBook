## Purpose

The Hospitality Coordinator performs a final readiness check on VIP rooms and any room flagged after a maintenance job, before the guest arrives.

## Room status definitions

| Status | Meaning | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|
| Dirty | Guest departed, not yet cleaned | System (on checkout) |
| Clean | Cleaned by Housekeeping | Room attendant |
| Inspected | Checked by Housekeeping supervisor | HK supervisor |
| Ready | Final check done; OK to assign | Hospitality Coordinator |
| Out of Order | Cannot be sold | Maintenance / Duty Manager |

Only assign rooms in **Inspected** or **Ready** status.

## Final check list (VIP / post-maintenance rooms)

1. Door and lock work; key card programmed for the correct dates.
2. Lights, TV, thermostat, and Wi-Fi function.
3. Bathroom: no hair, no drips, towels folded to standard, amenities full.
4. Bed: crisp, no visible wrinkles, correct pillow count (4 for king, 2 per bed for doubles).
5. No odors; windows and balcony door locked.
6. Welcome amenity and card in place for VIPs.
7. Mini-bar stocked and sealed.

## If something fails

- Minor (a burnt-out bulb, missing amenity): fix or request immediately, room stays **Inspected**.
- Major (AC not cooling, plumbing, safety issue): set to **Out of Order**, open a Maintenance ticket, and pick a new room for the guest.

## Documentation

Log the check on the task board with the room number, time, and result. A room without a logged check is treated as not checked.
