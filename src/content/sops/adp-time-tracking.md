## Purpose
Where you clock in and out, log breaks and lunch, and submit time off: ADP.

## What ADP is used for

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Clock in at the start of your shift | Sign in at https://signin.adp.com/ (the Location Coordinator day starts with a 7:45am log-in via the website) |
| Log breaks and lunch | ADP app on your cell phone, or the website |
| Clock out | ADP app or website |
| Submit time off | ADP app (and also email Abby; see the Time Off PTO SOP) |

## Steps

1. Go to https://signin.adp.com/ (bookmark it) or open the ADP app on your phone.
2. Sign in with your ADP credentials (**see 1Password**; your account is set up as part of payroll onboarding with Ben and Peri).
3. Clock in when you start, log breaks and lunch as they happen, and clock out when you finish.

> **Warning:** The Employee Handbook requires all non-exempt employees to enter actual hours on the company time clock in real time (not in advance, not delayed). Entering time for someone else or falsifying hours leads to discipline up to and including termination. Time clock updates are due before 8:00am on the Monday before payday.
