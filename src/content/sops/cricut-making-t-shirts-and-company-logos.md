Part of getting to know the team: use the Cricut to make your own Thinkspace shirt and company logos.

## What to do

> **Note for the trainer:** the Trello card only says "Make your own shirt!". Show the new coordinator where the Cricut, blanks and logo files live, and walk through the cut-and-press steps in person.
