# Power & Internet troubleshooting (Redmond) — 1EAwLJ8LXvsbQWH0JU7-mT3IwB9M0f5YT2keeVYY2kDo
In the event of a power outage from high wind, to check whether the Redmond building has power — follow ALL steps; no single one guarantees the answer:
- Look at the PSE Outage Map (pse.com/en/outage/outage-map). Check if the building is in the outage area. Not 100% foolproof.
- Check iVU (ivu.thinkspace.com). It uses both power and Internet — if the site doesn't come up it could be loss of either.
- Check Arlo Cameras (my.arlo.com). Uses both power and Internet — no video could mean loss of power or Internet.
- Check the Ziply outage map (ziplyfiber.com helpcenter → internet outage). May tell you whether there's an Internet outage in the area.
- Check the Xfinity Outage Map (xfinity.com/support/statusmap).
- Log in to Brivo (access.brivo.com). You can log in but will see com errors connecting to the controllers — Brivo is down and there may be a power outage.
