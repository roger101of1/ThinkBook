## Ethos, Mindset, and Principles — A Co-Committed Workplace
Co-Commitment means we each take 100% responsibility for our own experience, growth, and impact. We support each other's creativity and success — without controlling, rescuing, blaming, or seeking approval. We choose co-commitment because we are building a race crew, not a rescue crew.

In a Co-Committed Workplace:
- We speak authentically, even when it's hard.
- We listen generously, with curiosity over judgment.
- We encourage each other's fullest potential without sacrificing our own integrity.
- We are responsible *to* each other, not *for* each other.

### What Entanglements Are
Entanglements happen when unconscious patterns — control, blame, guilt, approval-seeking, rescuing — replace clear agreements and open communication. They erode trust and make work about personalities and approval instead of excellence and shared purpose.

You might hear: "If they change, then I can be okay." (Control) · "They make me feel this way." (Blame) · "I feel bad if I succeed and they don't." (Guilt) · "I can't say that, I might upset them." (Approval-seeking) · "I have to fix this for them or they'll fail." (Rescuing)

### Gossip (Entanglement) vs Co-Commitment (High Trust)
| | Gossip | Co-Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Talking *about* someone instead of *to* them | Speaking directly and respectfully to the person involved |
| Intention | Seek approval, vent, avoid discomfort | Solve problems openly, strengthen trust |
| Energy | Blame, resentment, division | Ownership, curiosity, unity |
| Peer to peer | "Can you believe what they did?" (to a third party) | "Hey, can we talk? I noticed something on my mind." |
| Leadership | "I don't know why leadership made that decision. It's stupid." (to coworkers) | "I'd like to understand the decision — could we talk about it?" (to leadership) |
| Escalation | Whispering concerns to coworkers | Requesting a one-on-one with the person or leader directly |
| Undermining | "The managers are out of touch. Not worth trying." | "I noticed a gap between what's happening and what's expected. Can we bridge it?" |
| Impact | Weakens trust, silos, fear | Builds trust, ownership, resilience |

**What to do if anyone is gossiping:** 1. Name it: "Sounds like gossip." 2. Shut it down: "Let's stick to our agreement — direct conversation." 3. Offer help: "How can I support you in bringing it up to them?"

### Our Framework for Feedback (RLFH — Reinforcement Learning from Humans)
1. **Consent:** "Can I provide you with some feedback?"
2. **Method:** "How would you best like to receive the feedback?" — **Coach** (you have the inner wisdom; I'll ask questions to draw it out), **Mentor** (I'll share experiences from similar situations; take what resonates), **Teacher** (here are the steps: 1, 2, 3…).
3. Another way is to ask the person to put on their beginner's-mindset hat.

## When we know someone is ready for promotion
Mastery within their role; consistently meets and exceeds expectations; an A player; great cultural fit; can train others; leadership qualities; takes ownership of their own growth; studies the job description they want and actively learns the skills; asks their manager "I want specific growth in (Leadership, Operations, Sales) — what are the ways I can get that?" or "What's the biggest challenge you're working on and how can I help?"

## When we know someone is no longer a fit — non-negotiable standards
**Work Performance and Responsibility:** failing to meet clearly defined goals; leaving your post unattended without approval beyond scheduled breaks/lunch; failing to notify the team when taking unexpected time off or leaving early; repeated lateness, absenteeism, or abuse of PTO.
**Trust and Integrity:** losing company property (keys, equipment) through negligence; lying about work completed; stealing from the company.
**Culture and Team Fit:** refusing to participate in Daily Huddles, Skills and Levels training, and operational improvements; violating the Zero Gossip policy; being technically strong but unwilling to collaborate; poor customer service that damages the brand.
**Refusing to Work On-Site:** We are a place-based company; our members are inside our spaces. Working physically from our spaces is required, not optional. Remote work is limited and must be approved.
**Our Philosophy:** We trade value for value — pay, benefits, community, opportunity for your time, energy, and best effort.
