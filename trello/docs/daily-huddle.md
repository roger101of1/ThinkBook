# DAILY HUDDLE FORMAT — 15euMCw-Mk6uoTCoFo2qE5Yh5MewfkTw98v6GfMuw_q0
Start Time: Daily 8:20 AM (8:20–8:30, starts promptly).

We show up on time and are not late because we respect each other's time. If a meeting is running long, invite the person you're meeting with to the Daily Huddle and explain it's the most important meeting of the day — our one time together; togetherness is a core value.

Each person's share is limited to 60 seconds; we want to hear from everyone. Come prepared — we are professionals, not amateurs. A designated person is the timer. We do not interrupt or ask clarifying questions until everyone has shared. Optional parking lot after the call to discuss speed bumps; if a speed bump is outside your expertise you can drop off. We are here to celebrate the wins!

1. **Wins** — something significant you worked on in the last business day that moved the company forward. (Examples: mind-mapped core values and a purpose statement; shared core values with the team; updated list of potential investors.) Did you make a connection? A win from your monthly goals?
2. **Speed bumps** — what's stopping you from getting work done, or customers having issues. (Examples: juggling 18 projects plus caregiving; chasing Company X for late rent; Property Management unresponsive.) Why? What's holding you back?
3. **Gratitude** — appreciation for someone on or off the team who did an extraordinary job or helped the company that day. (Examples: Kalen for showing me Miro; David Grant for sending the LLC agreement; Paige for the construction schedule.)
4. **WOD / Intention Word** — set your intention for the day in a word; how you'll focus on your SMART goals. Not the weather. (Examples: Inspired, Plowing, Optimistic.) A word list is linked from the doc.
