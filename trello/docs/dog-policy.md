## Doc A · Dog Policy Addendum (member contract addendum) — 150ssv4l271mhkOQqVs69586aass2oS7SR-QiekuWGnI
As a pet owner, you agree to pay a one-time refundable pet fee of $300. We require a photograph of the dog, vaccine certification, and spay/neuter certification for your tenant file. Thinkspace reserves the right to restrict the right to bring your pet at any time at our discretion. (Initial)

Your dog may be excluded if it: causes any person allergic reactions, fear, or physical/psychological discomfort; causes distraction or liability to other tenants; fails proper grooming and hygiene; fails to comply with the building's dog policy. (Initial)

The following dogs may not be brought to the workplace: restricted breeds or mixes — Pitbull Terriers, Mastiffs, Rottweilers, Staffordshire Terriers, Chow Chows, Presa Canarios, Wolf-hybrid, Cane Corso (a licensed vet may be required to verify breed); dogs not spayed/neutered; unvaccinated or sick dogs; dogs with internal or external parasites; dogs older than 6 months not spayed/neutered; dogs that jump, chase, bite, growl, bark, or behave aggressively; dogs that foul the inside or outside of the building.

All dogs must be leashed at all times and in continuous full control of their owners. Dogs are only allowed on the premises in the physical presence of the owner, and only in the owner's office. Dogs are not permitted in common areas — kitchen areas, meeting rooms, lobbies. Leashed and under control in hallways, stairways, elevators. Owners clean up completely and immediately. Owner covers in full any costs from non-compliance: building damage, cleaning fees, excessive pet hair, odor removal. (Initial)

Owner is completely and solely liable for injuries or damage caused by the animal. Repair/cleaning/maintenance costs, including failure to clean up waste within property lines, are charged in full to the next invoice, not against the pet deposit. Thinkspace is not liable for loss of or injury to any animal. Owner unconditionally releases Thinkspace from liability. Form fields: Dog's name / breed / age / owner name / signature / date.

## Doc C · Employees that want to bring in their dogs + member pet rules — 1sCHlafCbhuWzGgDstsya_zG4xjnesB8ArrrA6bs3IuU
Thinkspace loves dogs and is dog friendly, but:
- Do not feed your dog at the front desk (the food smells).
- Bringing a dog is once-in-a-blue-moon / emergency only — most of the time NOT at the front desk. Even when covering in an emergency, still ask your manager.
- Dogs must be well behaved, potty trained, and quiet.
- Dogs cannot go on sales tours.
- No dogs under two years of age.
- **Ask your manager for permission each time** before bringing your dog.

For members to participate, pets must be: at least 2 years old; up to date on state-required vaccinations; free of communicable infections and parasites; clean and well groomed; house-trained; obedient, well-socialized, no history of biting, barking, chasing, aggression; microchipped; covered under owner's homeowners/renters insurance that covers dog bites.

Pet owner responsibilities: 100% responsible for behavior, well-being, hygiene; respectful of others; pet-proofed workspace; keep the pet with them and controlled (or arrange a colleague); provide own leash/crate/gate, waste bags, toys, bowls; bathroom breaks, water, food, exercise; clean up accidents immediately; maintain dog-bite insurance; legally and financially responsible for damage; annual proof of vaccination/health; keep pets out of pet-free areas; use alternate care on days they can't manage the pet (all-day meeting) or the pet is ill; take the pet home if it becomes a distraction, nuisance, or danger, or if asked by their manager.

Managing issues: 1. Talk with the pet owner and resolve mutually. 2. Escalate to the pet owner's supervisor. 3. Escalate to Human Resources.
